RIE Electric Discussion

SYNOPSIS: Very dry conditions with abundant wildfire fuels will continue and the wildfire potential will remain elevated for both Capital and Coastal from today through the 14th. The most consequential period is today and tomorrow when the EEI Wildfire level reaches 4 (relatively high) in both regions. Minimum relative humidities will be below 30% the next couple days, with windy conditions. Both Capital and Coastal could see wind gusts at 40+mph through Thursday.
