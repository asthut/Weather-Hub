WHAT IT IS

Forward-looking outage intelligence

Storm Impact Analytics uses weather intelligence, machine learning, and utility-specific outage history to predict where and how severely upcoming weather will impact the electrical system, giving utilities actionable insight before outages occur.

HOW THE MODEL IS GENERATED

1

Build history

Align prior jobs with weather reanalysis, assets, land use and vegetation.

2

Machine learning identifies how the utility system responded to past hazards.

3

High-resolution weather models drive expected outage scale and geography.

4

Publish

DTN meteorologists review events and communicate forecast confidence.

CUSTOMER INPUTS

OMS history

3+ years optimal: Job ID, timing, customers affected, cause, device location, coordinates and time zone.

Operating geographies

Service territory, HQ, WMC and system boundaries, plus required prediction levels.

Distribution GIS

Overhead lines, poles, protective devices and transformers, including IDs and projection information.

Business rules

Storm tiers, incident thresholds, event triggers, restoration targets and authorized users.
